1. The Vision: Moving Beyond Forensic Bookkeeping

Every reconciliation tool in the market today does the same thing: it waits for money to move, then compares records afterward to find what went wrong. It is forensic. It explains the past. FinScope AI exists to end that.

Because FinScope AI lives inside Razorpay's payment rail, it does not wait for a bank settlement file to arrive days later. It watches money at the moment of authorization, at retry, and at every failure code returned by the payment switch.

The Core Shift

Traditional systems treat reconciliation as an accounting task performed days later. FinScope AI treats reconciliation as an autonomous control loop operating in milliseconds.

2. The Payment Rail Nervous System

FinScope AI unifies three separate layers into a single real-time ledger graph:

  1. Gateway Layer: Real-time authorizations, bank response codes, retry storms, and webhooks.
  2. Settlement Layer: Bank UTR numbers, batch processing files, settlement fees, and date lags.
  3. Internal Ledger Layer: ERP booking entries, accounting reference IDs, and double-entry accounts.
When an anomaly is detected at the gateway layer (e.g. 8 consecutive retries for the same customer), the nervous rail immediately warns the settlement layer before payouts are disbursed.

3. 4-Agent Autonomous Architecture

FinScope AI is driven by four coordinated agents:

1. Reconciliation Agent

Applies mathematical zero-hallucination rules first (resolving exact matches, known date lags, and rounding errors with zero AI cost), delegating only true edge-case exceptions to Claude.

2. Anomaly Watch Agent

Continuously inspects transaction streams for duplicate payout suspects, refund cascades, retry storms, and settlement delays, assigning a systemic Risk Score (0–100).

3. Cash Flow Forecast Agent

Projects 14-day forward liquidity based on pending settlement lags and historical gateway conversion rates, generating proactive shortfall alerts.

4. Financial Copilot

An interactive natural language intelligence layer that answers complex financial questions with direct citations grounded in live audit records.

4. Hardcoded Confidence & Value Guardrails

AI agents must never have unchecked authority over financial funds. FinScope AI enforces deterministic code-level guardrails:

Rule 1: Maximum Transaction Value Cap (₹50,000)
Any transaction exceeding ₹50,000 CANNOT be auto-approved by AI, regardless of 100% confidence. It must be escalated to a human controller.
Rule 2: Minimum Confidence Threshold (0.70)
If the AI diagnosis confidence falls below 0.70, auto-approval is strictly intercepted and flagged for human review.

5. Self-Calibrating Trust Tiers

Rather than granting universal autonomy, FinScope AI partitions autonomy by mismatch type:

Trust LevelAutonomy StateCriteria
Tier 1 (Provisional)Human review mandatory for all pairsDefault for new or volatile mismatch types
Tier 2 (Supervised)Auto-approved below ₹50,000; spot checksRequires 5 consecutive successful validations
Tier 3 (Autonomous)Full autonomous approval within safety capsRequires sustained human feedback concordance

6. Anomaly Watch Patterns & Detection

FinScope AI tracks 5 primary threat vectors in the Razorpay payment rail:

  • Duplicate Payout Suspects: Same vendor, identical amount within 48 hours without distinct reference.
  • Retry Storms: Customers or bots attempting >3 repeated payments for a single order.
  • Refund Clusters: Sudden concentration of chargebacks or refunds indicating merchant fraud.
  • Decline Drift: Bank-level spike in specific error codes (e.g. `BANK_TIMEOUT`).
  • Settlement Delays: High-value transactions remaining unsettled past standard T+2 SLA.
